# TRIGORA — Revenue scenarios

These are **sensitivity scenarios, not forecasts or guarantees**. Revenue depends on partner agreements, signal quality, click/lead rates and conversion.

| Stage | detected events/mo | monetized share | avg revenue/monetized event | monthly gross revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pilot | 250 | 6% | €15 | €225 |
| Traction | 1,000 | 8% | €18 | €1,440 |
| Scale 1 | 3,000 | 10% | €20 | €6,000 |
| Scale 2 | 10,000 | 10% | €20 | €20,000 |
| Network | 25,000 | 12% | €22 | €66,000 |

## Interpretation
The target is not to maximize traffic. The target is to increase **revenue per detected event** by adding better matching and more monetization paths per event.

## Cost discipline
Keep fixed software spend at €0 until a real bottleneck appears. First paid items should be the domain and minimal operational tooling only after the first measurable revenue.
